# ShopEase Shipping Policy

## Overview

ShopEase delivers orders across standard serviceable postal codes. Orders are processed once payment authorization and order details are confirmed. Customers can track their package status through their ShopEase account under the order history section. Standard dispatch usually occurs within 1 to 2 business days after an order is placed. Delivery timelines may vary depending on the destination, selected shipping method, and local carrier operations.

## Shipping Methods & Timelines

Standard shipping typically delivers orders within 3 to 7 business days following dispatch. Express shipping options may be available for select locations and product categories for faster delivery. Delivery timelines are estimates and may be subject to delays during sale events, festive periods, or severe weather conditions. Customers will receive a tracking link via email or SMS once the shipment is dispatched.

## Delivery & Tracking

Customers should ensure that accurate shipping addresses and contact details are provided at checkout to prevent delivery failures. If a package is marked as delivered but cannot be located, customers should check with household members or building reception before contacting support. Undeliverable packages returned to ShopEase due to incorrect address details may require address verification prior to re-dispatch.
