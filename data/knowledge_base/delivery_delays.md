# ShopEase Delivery Delays Policy

## Overview

ShopEase strives to deliver all orders within the estimated delivery timelines provided at checkout. However, unforeseen logistics constraints, operational challenges, or external factors can occasionally lead to shipping delays.

## Common Causes of Delay

Delivery delays may occur due to severe weather conditions, local transport strikes, regional holidays, high-volume sale events, or incomplete delivery addresses. When a delay occurs, the logistics carrier automatically reschedules the delivery for the next available business day.

## Delay Management & Escalation

Customers can monitor real-time shipping updates using the tracking link in their account. If an order is delayed beyond 3 business days past the estimated delivery date, customers can contact ShopEase support to initiate a carrier investigation. If the shipment is confirmed lost or indefinitely delayed, a full refund or free replacement will be issued immediately.
