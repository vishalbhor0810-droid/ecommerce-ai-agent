# ShopEase Failed Payments Policy

## Overview

A failed payment occurs when a transaction cannot be processed successfully during checkout due to bank network issues, incorrect payment details, expired credentials, or insufficient funds. If a payment fails, the order status will remain unpaid or unconfirmed, and no items will be dispatched.

## Deducted Amounts & Auto-Refunds

If funds are debited from a customer's bank account or card for an order that fails at checkout, the amount is held securely by the payment gateway or bank. In most cases, the debited amount is automatically reversed to the original payment method within 3 to 5 business days without requiring manual intervention.

## Troubleshooting & Next Steps

Customers experiencing a payment failure should verify their payment details, ensure sufficient account balance, and check bank connectivity. If the transaction fails repeatedly, customers are advised to try an alternate payment method (such as UPI or COD) or contact their issuing bank. If the debited amount is not refunded after 5 business days, customers can contact ShopEase support with the transaction reference number for assistance.
