# ShopEase Exchange Policy

## Overview

ShopEase allows customers to request a product exchange within 7 days of delivery for issues related to sizing, color preferences, or defective items. Replacement items are issued subject to stock availability at the time the request is processed.

## Exchange Eligibility & Process

To initiate an exchange, customers should navigate to "My Orders," select the delivered product, and choose the "Exchange" option. Customers must specify the desired size or replacement variant. The original product must be unused, unwashed, and handed over with all original tags, accessories, and packaging intact.

## Out of Stock & Price Differences

If the requested size or item is out of stock, a full refund or store credit will be offered instead following standard return inspection. If the replacement item has a higher price, the customer will be required to pay the price difference prior to dispatch. If the replacement item is lower in price, the excess amount will be refunded to the original payment method.
