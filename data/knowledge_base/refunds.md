# ShopEase Refund Policy

## Overview

ShopEase processes refunds for approved returned items, cancelled orders, or failed transactions. Once a returned product passes quality inspection at our warehouse, the refund process is initiated automatically. The refund amount will cover the purchase price of the item, along with any applicable taxes paid during checkout. Shipping fees may be non-refundable depending on the reason for return.

## Refund Timelines & Methods

Refunds are issued back to the original payment method used during order placement. For credit card, debit card, and net banking transactions, refunds typically reflect in the account within 5 to 7 business days after processing. For Cash on Delivery (COD) orders, refunds are credited via bank transfer or ShopEase store credit as selected by the customer.

## Failed & Pending Refunds

If a refund does not reflect in the customer's account after the specified processing period, the customer should contact their bank using the provided refund transaction ID. In cases where payment was deducted for an unsuccessful order, the amount is automatically refunded by the payment gateway within 3 to 5 business days. Customers can contact ShopEase support if further assistance is needed.
