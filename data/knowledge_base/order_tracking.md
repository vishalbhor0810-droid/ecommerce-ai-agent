# ShopEase Order Tracking Policy

## Overview

ShopEase provides real-time order tracking for all shipped purchases. Once an order is dispatched, customers receive a confirmation notification via email and SMS containing a unique tracking ID and a direct carrier link. Tracking information allows customers to monitor the package status from the warehouse to their delivery address.

## Tracking Process

To track an order, customers should log in to their ShopEase account, navigate to "My Orders," and select the specific order. Clicking the "Track Order" button displays current location updates, estimated delivery dates, and handling status. Customers can also use the tracking ID directly on the logistics partner's portal for additional details.

## Tracking Status Issues

Tracking details may take up to 24 hours to update on the carrier system after a shipping label is created. If the tracking status shows no updates for more than 3 business days or indicates a delivery issue, customers should reach out to ShopEase support for investigation.
