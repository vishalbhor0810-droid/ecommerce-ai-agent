# ShopEase Order Cancellation Policy

## Overview

ShopEase allows customers to cancel an order as long as it has not yet been processed for shipment. Customers can check the cancellation eligibility of their order directly from their account under the order management section. Once an order enters the shipping or dispatch stage, it can no longer be cancelled directly through the app or website.

## Cancellation Process

To cancel an eligible order, customers should open their order history, select the active order, and click on the cancel option. A cancellation reason must be selected from the provided options before submitting the request. If the order is successfully cancelled before dispatch, a full refund including any paid delivery charges will be initiated automatically.

## Post-Dispatch Options

If an order has already been shipped and cannot be cancelled, customers may refuse delivery when the courier arrives. Alternatively, customers can accept the delivery and initiate a standard return request following the ShopEase Return Policy. Orders containing personalized items or cleared non-refundable products cannot be cancelled once production or processing begins.
