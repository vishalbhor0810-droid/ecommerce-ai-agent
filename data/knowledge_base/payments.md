# ShopEase Payment Policy

## Overview

ShopEase supports multiple secure payment methods to offer a convenient checkout experience for all customers. Accepted payment methods include major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, American Express), Net Banking, Unified Payments Interface (UPI), digital wallets, and Cash on Delivery (COD). All online transactions are processed through encrypted, PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways to ensure maximum data security.

## Payment Verification

For security purposes, certain transactions may undergo a brief verification step by the card issuer or payment provider. Orders are confirmed only after full payment authorization is received by ShopEase. If a payment is flagged for fraud prevention or unauthorized activity, ShopEase reserves the right to hold or cancel the order until verification is completed.

## Price & Currency

All product prices listed on ShopEase are inclusive of applicable taxes unless stated otherwise. Prices are displayed in Indian Rupees (INR). Final charges, including item price, applicable taxes, and shipping fees, are clearly itemized on the final checkout screen prior to payment confirmation.
