# ShopEase Warranty Policy

## Overview

ShopEase offers warranty coverage on select product categories, such as electronics and home appliances, against manufacturing defects. Warranty terms, duration, and scope vary by manufacturer and product brand. Specific coverage details are mentioned on the product detail page and invoice.

## Coverage & Exclusions

Warranty covers functional defects, hardware failures, and component malfunctions occurring under normal operating conditions. It does not cover physical damage, liquid ingress, unauthorized repairs, normal wear and tear, or accidental damage caused by customer misuse.

## Claiming Warranty

To claim a warranty, customers must retain their purchase invoice as proof of purchase. For brand-serviced products, claims can be submitted directly through the manufacturer's authorized service center. For ShopEase-backed warranties, customers can initiate a claim via the "My Orders" section or contact customer support for repair or replacement guidance.
