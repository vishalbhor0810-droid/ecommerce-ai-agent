# ShopEase Cash on Delivery (COD) Policy

## Overview

ShopEase offers Cash on Delivery (COD) as a flexible payment option for eligible postal codes and product categories. COD allows customers to pay for their order in cash or via digital payment upon physical delivery.

## Conditions & Limits

COD availability is determined at checkout based on pin code serviceability, order value limits, and seller restrictions. High-value orders or custom items may require prepaid payment methods. Customers choosing COD are requested to keep exact change or an active UPI application ready at the time of delivery to ensure a smooth handover.

## Refusal & Policy Enforcement

Repeated refusal or non-acceptance of COD orders upon delivery may result in the temporary or permanent disablement of the COD payment option for the customer's account. In cases of COD returns, refunds are credited via bank transfer or ShopEase store credit once the returned item passes quality inspection.
