# ShopEase Customer Support Policy

## Overview

ShopEase is committed to delivering responsive, helpful, and efficient support for all customer inquiries, order updates, and service requests. Our customer support channels are designed to provide assistance across account management, order status, payments, returns, and refunds.

## Support Channels & Hours

Customers can reach out to ShopEase support through the following channels:
- **In-App & Web Chat:** Integrated automated assistant and live support available 24/7.
- **Email Support:** Submit inquiries via the contact form or email support@shopease.com. Standard response time is within 24 business hours.
- **Helpline:** Customer support phone assistance available Monday through Saturday from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM IST.

## Escalation & Resolution Time

Most standard inquiries—such as order tracking, cancellation requests, or basic return queries—are resolved instantly or within 24 hours. For complex cases involving bank refund trace IDs, lost shipments, or warranty verification, escalations may take 2 to 5 business days. Customers are provided with a unique ticket reference number to track the status of their support request until fully resolved.
