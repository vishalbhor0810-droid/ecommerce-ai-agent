# ShopEase Damaged & Defective Products Policy

## Overview

ShopEase ensures that all items are inspected and securely packaged before dispatch. In the rare event that a package arrives damaged, tampered with, or defective, customers are entitled to a hassle-free replacement or full refund.

## Reporting Window & Proof Requirements

Customers must report damaged, missing, or incorrect items within 48 hours of delivery. To report an issue, customers should navigate to "My Orders," select the affected product, and submit clear photos or an unboxing video showing the shipping label, packaging, and the specific defect or damage.

## Resolution & Pickup

Upon verification by the ShopEase support team, a reverse pickup will be scheduled. Once the damaged item is collected or verified, a free replacement will be dispatched, or a full refund will be processed immediately to the original payment method.
