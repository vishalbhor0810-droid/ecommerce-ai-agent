# ShopEase Privacy Policy

## Overview

ShopEase is committed to protecting customer privacy and personal data. This policy outlines how customer information is collected, stored, used, and safeguarded when using our platform and customer service channels.

## Information Collection & Usage

We collect necessary personal information such as name, contact details, delivery address, and payment information to process orders, facilitate deliveries, and provide customer support. Customer data may also be used to send transaction updates, order receipts, and relevant promotional communications if opted in.

## Data Protection & Third-Party Sharing

ShopEase implements industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect user data from unauthorized access or disclosure. Personal details are shared only with trusted third-party partners (such as logistics carriers and payment gateways) strictly necessary to fulfill services. We do not sell or rent customer data to third parties. Customers can request data access or account deletion by contacting ShopEase support.
